Monsterlings — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Monsterlings does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No ads, no purchases. The game is free and self-contained.
  • Local scores only. Your best score and progress are stored on your device and never leave it.

When online, the app may download game-content updates from a static web host (GitHub Pages). This is a plain file download: no personal information is sent by the app, and we receive no data about you. The host may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) as any web server does; we have no access to and make no use of such logs.
